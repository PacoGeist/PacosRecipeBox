# Release notes

Every version of Recipe Box, newest first.

**How to add a new version:** before tagging a release, add a section at the top, below this note. Its heading must start with `## ` and the version number, as in `## 1.1.0 — Month Day, Year`. When the version tag is pushed, the release workflow copies that section onto the GitHub release page.

---

## 1.0.0 — November 2026

**Recipe Box** is a free, offline recipe app for Android phones and tablets, and for iPhone and iPad as a web app.

> **Moving from an earlier test version?** This release is signed with a new key, so Android can't install it over the old app. In the old app, open **Settings ▸ Back up all my recipes** and save the backup file somewhere you can reach from the phone. Then uninstall the old app, install this one, and use **Settings ▸ Restore from a backup** to bring back your recipes, favorites, notes, menus and grocery lists. On iPhone and iPad nothing changes: the web app updates by itself and keeps your recipes.

### Recipes
- **590 built-in recipes**: 50 for each of 12 cuisines (American, Mexican, Japanese, Chinese, British, Irish, German, Indian, Native American, BBQ, Slow Cooker and Potluck). Each recipe has a photo and credits its author and source.
- **Recipe pages** show:
  - a photo of the finished dish
  - prep, cook and total time
  - servings
  - **½× 1× 2× 3×** buttons that rescale every ingredient
  - ingredients you can check off as you go
  - step photos
- **Make every recipe your own:**
  - edit any recipe, including the built-in ones
  - keep your own notes on it
  - tap **I made this** to record the days you cooked it
- **Add your own recipes** with photos from the camera or gallery.
- **Import recipes from websites** by pasting a link.

### Finding recipes
- **Cookbook-cover opening screen** and a colorful home page with buttons for:
  - My & Family Recipes, Friends' Recipes, Internet Recipes and Favorites
  - Cuisines, Seasonal (seasons and holidays), By Author and All Recipes
  - every meal type
- **Cuisine, season and holiday pages** show one button per meal type, plus Favorites and All.
- **Favorites come first** in every recipe list. Other sorts are A to Z, Quickest and Newest.
- **Search** by name, keyword, author or ingredient, with filters for cuisine, meal type, season, holiday, collection, time and favorites.

### Plan & shop
- **Menu Planner:** pick several recipes for a day, add a note for the day, and tick off each dish as you make it. Every recipe page also has an **Add to menu** button.
- **Grocery lists** from a whole menu or from a single recipe, scaled to the size you picked. Items are grouped by store department:
  - Produce
  - Meat & Seafood
  - Dairy & Eggs
  - Bakery
  - Dry Goods & Canned: anything canned counts as dry goods
  - Frozen
- **Shopping with a list:** checked items move to an "In the cart" section at the bottom. You can also:
  - add your own items
  - move an item to another department
  - share the list as text
- **Grocery List** button on the home page opens your latest list. **All My Lists** shows every list you've made.
- **Menu History:** a calendar of what you planned and what you cooked each day.

### Sharing, backup and settings
- **Share recipes** as a PDF recipe card, printed, as text, on Facebook, or as a `.recipe` file another Recipe Box user can open.
- **Back up and restore** in one file: your recipes, photos, favorites, notes, menus and grocery lists.
- **Settings:**
  - light, dark or automatic mode
  - custom colors
  - five fonts
  - text sizes
  - an About page with the version and credits
- **Update button:** downloads and installs the newest Android version from GitHub.
- **iPhone and iPad:** open https://pacogeist.github.io/PacosRecipeBox/ in Safari, then tap Share ▸ Add to Home Screen. It works offline.
